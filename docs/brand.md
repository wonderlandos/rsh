# rsh brand

## The mark: the cut prompt

A `>` prompt carved like a stone, with a cut stone where the cursor blinks. The prompt says "shell", the stone says "Ruby", and the two sit on one line the way the two languages sit in one script. Each arm of the `>` is cut into two facets that meet on a ridge, lit from the upper left, in the same steps of white as the Cryswol gem (full, 84 %, 72 %, 50 %). It sits on the same rounded badge as Cryswol (radius 230 on a 1024 grid), so the WolOS language marks read as one family: one bold white glyph on one colour field.

It copies no language mark: it has no gem silhouette of its own, no crown, no letters. It is not a mascot and it has no pixel detail; at 16 px it is redrawn flat.

## Colour

Every colour is a WolOS token (`design/tokens/colors.json` of the WolOS repository), plus one accent of rsh's own.

| Name | Value | From |
| --- | --- | --- |
| rsh garnet (accent) | `#A51C55` | rsh |
| light red | `#CF3A68` | `light.red` |
| dark red | `#F2678F` | `dark.red` |
| tint | `#F7AFC3` | `icon.categories.talk.tint` |
| ink | `#181521` | `light.text` |

| dark tile | `#2A2832` | `dark.surface` |
| quiet text on dark | `#AAA7B6` | `dark.text-2` |

The badge is a diagonal from dark red through light red to garnet, the way the Cryswol badge runs from orange to red.

### Why garnet: three options

1. **Garnet `#A51C55` on the WolOS reds (chosen).** A deep red leaning to purple: it reads as a red stone, so "Ruby" comes through without the colour of any existing Ruby mark, and its lean to purple ties it to the WolOS violet `#7C5CFF`. It sits between Cryswol (orange to red, hue about 10 to 25) and WolOS (violet, hue about 250), at hue about 335.
2. **WolOS violet to light red (`#8B6CFF` to `#CF3A68`).** Ties rsh hard to the system. Against: next to WolOS app icons it reads as one more WolOS app, not as a language with its own name, and the violet half carries nothing of Ruby.
3. **Terminal night: the dark tile `#221B36` with a mint glyph `#5BE3B0`.** Reads "shell" at once. Against: it says nothing of Ruby, and it looks like every terminal icon already in the dock.

### Premortem of garnet

Imagine it failed a year from now. The likely reasons, and the answer to each:

- *Next to Cryswol it is one more red badge, and at 16 px nobody can tell them apart.* The hues are about 30 degrees apart and the glyph silhouettes differ (a closed cube against an open `>` and a small stone). The 16 px files are drawn for the grid so the open `>` survives. If they still blur, move garnet further to purple (`#8E1A63`), not toward red.
- *Red means "error" in a terminal.* The colour lives on the badge, the avatar and the social preview only. The interactive shell keeps the WolOS colours for its prompt and its warnings; rsh does not paint text garnet.
- *People read it as the Ruby logo.* The big shape is a `>`, not a stone; the stone is the cursor, under a third of the glyph's width, seen from the side with no crown. The garnet is pinker than the usual Ruby red.
- *White on the light end of the gradient is weak.* The glyph's lit facets are full white on `#F2678F` and darker below; the weakest pair (50 % white on garnet) is a shadow facet and needs no contrast of its own.

## Files

Everything in `brand/` is generated by `python3 -I brand/build.py` (needs `rsvg-convert`, `fontTools`, and the Inter and JetBrains Mono fonts for the outlined words). Every SVG is paths only: no text, no fonts, no images; the badge gradient is the only definition.

| File | Grid | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `rsh-badge.svg` | 1024 | The mark. Light and dark grounds alike. 24 px and up. |
| `rsh-badge-dark.svg` | 1024 | The stone on the dark tile, for dark docks and dark app lists. |
| `rsh-glyph.svg` | 1024 | Bare glyph in garnet and the reds, for light grounds (documents, print). |
| `rsh-glyph-dark.svg` | 1024 | Bare glyph lit for dark grounds. |
| `rsh-glyph-white.svg` | 1024 | Bare glyph in white facets, for photos and colour fields. |
| `rsh-glyph-mono.svg` | 1024 | One colour (ink), no facets: stamps, one-ink places. |
| `rsh-badge-16.svg`, `rsh-badge-16-dark.svg` | 16 | Drawn for 16 px: flat white `>` and stone. Never scale the 1024 badge to 16. |
| `rsh-symbolic.svg` | 16 | One path in `currentColor`, for menus and toolbars that recolour icons. |
| `rsh-wordmark.svg`, `rsh-wordmark-dark.svg` | 128 high | Badge and "rsh" (JetBrains Mono ExtraBold, outlined) for light and dark grounds. At least 160 px wide. |
| `rsh-social.svg` | 1280x640 | The repository's social preview. |
| `png/rsh-badge@1x.png` ... `@4x` | 128, 256, 512 | Raster badge; also `rsh-badge-dark`, `rsh-glyph`, `rsh-glyph-dark`. |
| `png/rsh-avatar-512.png` | 512 | Repository and organisation avatar. |
| `png/rsh-social-1280x640.png` | 1280x640 | Upload as the GitHub social preview. |
| `png/rsh-icon-16.png` ... `-64.png` | 16, 32, 48, 64 | Terminal and launcher icons (16 from the 16 px drawing); `rsh-icon-dark-*` for dark themes. |
| `png/rsh-wordmark@2x.png` | 256 high | Raster wordmark. |

## Rules

- Clear space around the badge is one fifth of its width.
- Below 24 px use the 16 px drawings or the symbolic glyph.
- Do not recolour the facets, add a shadow, stretch the badge or put the white glyph on a busy photo without its badge.
- The name is written `rsh` in running text and code, `RubyShell` when it needs saying aloud.
